Contact us

SciLifeLab Serve is developed and maintained by SciLifeLab, specifically the SciLifeLab Data Centre. SciLifeLab Data Centre staff members are distributed across all SciLifelab sites: Solna, Uppsala, Gothenburg, Lund, Umeå, Linköping. Below you can find a list of team members that are currently contributing to the SciLifeLab Serve platform.

You are welcome to approach us with questions on how to use our platform and requests for help if you get stuck following our user guide. We are also happy to give advice about your particular use case — for example, if you would like to share a machine learning model or a data science application and do not know where to begin, or if you are unsure whether SciLifeLab Serve meets your needs. We are always happy to hear suggestions on how we can improve our platform. All life science researchers affiliated with a Swedish research institute are welcome to approach us.

The best way to get in touch with us is to send an email to serve@scilifelab.se. We try to respond to emails fairly fast but keep in mind that we only work regular work days and hours.
