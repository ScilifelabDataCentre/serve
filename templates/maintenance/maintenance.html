Planned maintenance

On this page you can find the schedule of planned maintenance on the SciLifeLab Serve platform, including cluster upgrades and other work that may cause brief interruptions. Apps and other services may be temporarily unavailable during these windows. We typically start maintenance at the beginning of the day; while in most cases the maintenance is over within a few hours, depending on what we do, it may take up to the whole day. Besides the planned maintenance, sometimes we may have to perform some urgent work; in those cases we try to inform the users on top and bottom of SciLifeLab Serve pages.
